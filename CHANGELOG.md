## 0.6.0

First public release, for WoW Forever 1.60.1.

- Hover a recipe at any profession trainer to see the normal recipe
  tooltip and, beside it, the real item it makes, like any item tooltip.
  A gold arrow points from the recipe to the item.
- Blizzard's normal compare shows your equipped item right of the item,
  headed "Equipped". It's always on: no need to hold Shift. Nothing covers
  the recipe list.
- The compare shows for gear you can't use, too.
- With Auctionator, the recipe tooltip also shows what one craft's
  reagents cost, and the profit after the auction house cut (5%). Profit
  shows only when every price is known, and never for items that bind on
  pickup.
- "Only show usable gear" in the trainer's Settings dropdown fades gear
  you can't use (armor type, weapon skill, level, class). Recipe rows are
  never changed otherwise.
- Nothing to set up, and no slash commands.
- Icon in the addon list.
